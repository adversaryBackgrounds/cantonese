Before you learn here are some things that will be normal: It is easier to read chinese than to write it

If you dont know a word someone just say this!:

__ 係咩意思?
__ hai6 me1 ji3 si1?

Anyway, lets continue
So when you're talking to people, they will probably ask some basic formalities e.g.
What is your name?
你叫咩名啊?
lei5 giu3 me1 meng3 aa3?
WORD BY WORD ANALYSIS: you - call - what - name?

(Here, 啊, is just a sentence particle, it doesnt mean anything because its like a vocal stim, but you add it anyway)

In which you reply:
我係 ___.
Ngo5 hai6 ___

Being polite
If you want to politely decline say this:
唔使
m4 sai2

To say thank you:
唔該
And very thanks (expected when somebody gives you a gift, Do not ever say 唔該 to a gift):
多謝
do1 ze3

To say you're welcome! 唔使客氣 m4 sai2 haak3 hei3
To apologize:
You can say Sorry (in english)
or 對唔住 doi3 m4 zyu6 (sincrere)
or 唔好意思 m4 hou2 yi4 si4 (very light sorry, can be used as excuse me)

If you want to get through a crowd:
唔該借借
m4 goi1 ze3 ze3

Goodbye
拜拜 bai1 bai1
